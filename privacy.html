Score Keeper Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · App package: com.hostreadyllc.scorekeeper

Score Keeper is a scoreboard app published by hostreadyllc. This policy explains what happens to information you enter into the app.

What the app stores

The app stores the games you score, team names, player names and your saved teams. All of this stays on your device, in the app's own storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

Score Keeper does not create accounts, collect personal information, use analytics or advertising, track your location, or share data with third parties. It works without an internet connection and makes no network requests while you use it.

Deleting your data

You can clear any game with New game. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
