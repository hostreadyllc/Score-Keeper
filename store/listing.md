## Full description

Score Keeper is a scoreboard for 26 sports, and every sport is scored by its own rules. Pick a sport, tap to score, and the app handles the rest.

TIMED TEAM SPORTS
Basketball (NBA, FIBA and NCAA), soccer, American football, rugby union and league, ice hockey, field hockey, handball, netball, water polo, futsal, lacrosse and Australian rules. Each has its own scoring buttons, a game clock, quarters, halves or periods, overtime, and counters for fouls, cards and timeouts.

RALLY AND NET SPORTS
Volleyball and beach volleyball, badminton, table tennis, squash and pickleball. Set targets, win by two, badminton's 30-point cap, table tennis serve rotation and pickleball side-out scoring are all built in, with set point and match point alerts.

TENNIS AND PADEL
15, 30, 40, deuce and advantage or golden point, games, sets, 7-point tiebreaks and 10-point final-set tiebreaks.

CRICKET
Runs, wickets and overs for T20, ODI, T10 or no over limit. Record wides, no-balls, byes and leg-byes. During a chase you see the target, runs needed and required run rate.

BASEBALL AND SOFTBALL
Balls, strikes and outs, runners on base, walks that move runners along, and a full inning-by-inning line score.

GOLF
A scorecard for up to six players against par, with birdies circled and bogeys boxed, just like a paper card.

TEAMS AND PLAYERS
Name your teams and add your players. Saved teams can be loaded into any game. In team sports, credit each score to a player and see a scorers table.

BUILT FOR THE SIDELINE
- Works with no internet connection
- Undo for every tap
- The screen stays on while you score
- Your game is saved if you leave the app
- Light and dark themes
- No ads, no account, and no data leaves your phone
